How the score is calculated

For each option: weighted score = sum(weight × score) across every criterion, divided by the sum of the weights. That keeps the total on the same 1-10 scale as the inputs, so a criterion weighted 9 pulls roughly twice as hard on the result as one weighted 4-5.
